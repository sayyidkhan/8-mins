# Hackathon judging criteria

Three criteria are scored **1–5 by each judge**, weighted to 100.

| Weight | Criterion | Judging question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 30% | Concept | Is the idea clear, specific and yours — and does the format fit the message? |
| 40% | Message | Is it accurate, and would it actually shift what someone understands, how confident they feel talking about drugs, or whether they'd act? |
| 30% | Product | Does it work without you standing next to it, and would a stranger walking past want to try it? |

## Important notes

- **Message carries the most weight, deliberately.** A technically dazzling build that does not move anyone should not beat a simpler one that does.
- **Message is also a gate.** Anything factually wrong, or that makes drug use look appealing, gets flagged. A flagged build cannot place, however good it is. Ask a facilitator if unsure.
