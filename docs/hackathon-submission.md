## Your project, in 50 words

The Next 8 Seconds is an interactive storybook following Bob home. Armed with a magical sketchbook, players have eight seconds to draw helpful objects over scenes of drug offers and peer pressure. AI recognises their sketches and brings choices to life, helping Bob refuse drugs, seek support, and reach safety.

## Your message, in 50 words

You do not need drugs to belong, cope, or prove yourself. You can refuse an offer, leave a pressured situation, and ask someone trusted for help. Supporting a friend means listening without judgement and getting urgent help when needed. One practical action can be the start of a safer choice.
