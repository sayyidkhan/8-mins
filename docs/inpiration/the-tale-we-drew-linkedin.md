# Inspiration: *The Tale We Drew*

**Source:** [Yanwen Chen on LinkedIn — The Tale We Drew](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/yanwen-chen-1001001_gamedev-godot-aigames-ugcPost-7506541216788889600-pA47/)

## Why it is relevant

*The Tale We Drew* is a 100-hour, four-person playable storybook adventure and a finalist in the Tokyo AI (TAI) × OpenAI 100-Hour Game Builder Challenge.

Its central interaction is simple: players draw objects into a painted world; the system interprets their drawing, generates it in 3D, and lets that creation help the creatures they encounter. The resulting experience pairs AI drawing interpretation, 3D generation, character dialogue, and voice inside a Godot game world.

**Useful product principle:** “Draw something. Help someone.” The magic comes from the player seeing that a thing they created meaningfully changes the world.
